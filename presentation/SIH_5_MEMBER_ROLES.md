## 3. Tough Judge Questions & Winning Answers (National Track)

#### Q1: "How does this platform overcome state-center jurisdictional conflicts?"
- **Answer (Member 1):** *"Under the Disaster Management Act of 2005, the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) and the National Executive Committee (NEC) have statutory authority during inter-state and severe calamities. AegisAlert National provides a shared, common operational picture so state SDMAs and the Union Home Ministry work on identical live data without conflicting reports."*

#### Q2: "How does the Cell Broadcast feature work without internet on ordinary keypad phones?"
- **Answer (Member 3):** *"AegisAlert formats all emergency warnings into the ITU X.1303 Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) standard used by the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and C-DOT. When authorized, these packets flash directly onto all 2G/3G/4G/5G phone screens attached to nearby telecom towers without requiring any internet connection or phone numbers."*

#### Q3: "How does the system scale when millions of citizens use it simultaneously?"
- **Answer (Member 2 / Member 3):** *"Because it is an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA), static assets and audio oscillators are cached on client devices. Only lightweight, compressed binary distress payloads (under 100 bytes) are transmitted back to the server—enabling the infrastructure to handle millions of concurrent sessions with minimal bandwidth."*
