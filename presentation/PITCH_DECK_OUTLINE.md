# Smart India Hackathon (SIH): AegisAlert National 3-Minute Pitch Deck Outline
### *Apex Multi-Ministry Disaster Management Track (NDMA & Ministry of Home Affairs)*

**Format:** 8 Slides + 60-Second Live Multi-Portal Demonstration  
**Target Ministry:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), NDMA, Ministry of Defence (MoD), Ministry of Jal Shakti

---

### Slide 1: Title & National Mandate
- **Visual:** Aegis tactical shield with the Ashoka Chakra and satellite overlay of the Indian Subcontinent.
- **Headline:** AEGISALERT NATIONAL — Apex Multi-Ministry Disaster Early Warning & Life-Saving Command Grid.
- **Apex Authority:** National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA) & Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), New Delhi.

---

### Slide 2: The National Tragedy — Why Isolated State Apps Fail
- **Visual:** Split screen showing multi-state floods (Uttarakhand cloudburst feeding UP/Bihar; Bay of Bengal cyclones striking Odisha and Andhra simultaneously).
- **The Core Problem:**
  1. **Disasters Cross State Lines:** Fragmented state-level portals do not share upstream dam and river data.
  2. **Inter-Agency Delays:** Coordination between MHA, Defence (IAF/Army), Jal Shakti, and telecom is hindered by bureaucratic silos.
  3. **Communication Collapse:** In severe events, optical fiber snaps and cell towers fail, blinding emergency responders.
- **The Breakthrough:** A unified national command grid running 100% offline via PWA, connecting 7 Union Ministries, 16 NDRF Battalions, the Armed Forces, and all citizens with ₹0 hardware cost.

---

### Slide 3: The 7-Ministry Apex Integration & 4 Multidisciplinary Portals
- **Visual:** National organizational hierarchy connecting:
  - MHA (NDMA), MoD (Armed Forces HADR), Jal Shakti (CWC), MoES (IMD), DoT (Cell Broadcast), MDoNER, MoHFW.
- **The 4 Portals:**
  - **1. National Command War Room (NEOC):** Pan-India threat index, 5,300-dam safety monitoring, and pre-judgments.
  - **2. Citizen Survival Hub:** 1-Tap SOS, 8-Language Vernacular Voice SOS, and safe highland routing.
  - **3. NDRF & Armed Forces Console:** 16 NDRF Battalions, IAF Mi-17/Chinook sorties, and Army Bailey bridges.
  - **4. National Relief Shelter & Health:** Pan-India bed occupancy, drinking water buffer, and family reunification.

---

### Slide 4: Pan-India Theaters & AI Predictive Pre-Judgments
- **Visual:** Map showing the 4 National Disaster Theaters:
  - *Northern Himalayan (Cloudbursts/Landslides)*
  - *Eastern & North-Eastern (Brahmaputra/GLOF)*
  - *Western Peninsular (Western Ghats/Mumbai)*
  - *Southern & Eastern Coastal (Bay of Bengal Cyclones)*
- **Pre-Judgment AI:** Calculates dam crest overtopping and embankment breaches 20 to 60 minutes before casualties happen, issuing automated Cabinet-level Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).

---

### Slide 5: 8-Language Vernacular Voice SOS & Offline PWA
- **Visual:** Smartphone interface showing Voice SOS in Hindi, Assamese, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, and English.
- **Inclusivity Features:**
  - **PWA Service Worker:** Runs completely offline without internet or phone signal.
  - **Voice AI:** Illiterate citizens speak in their mother tongue; AI automatically extracts victim count and rooftop entrapment status.
  - **Sensory Alerts:** Blasts 120dB civil defense alarm through device speakers and flashes full-screen visual strobes for hearing-impaired citizens.

---

### Slide 6: Tri-Services Military HADR & National Relief Logistics
- **Visual:** Dual screen showing IAF helicopter tasking and national highland shelter bed counters.
- **Operational Capabilities:**
  - **One-Click Military Authorization:** Task IAF Mi-17 helicopters, Army engineering units, and Coast Guard patrol vessels.
  - **Vulnerability Triage Scoring (0 to 100):** Ensures pregnant mothers, infants, and elderly stranded in water are rescued first.
  - **National Family Reunification Portal:** Reconnects displaced families across state lines.

### Slide 7: Live Demonstration Sequence (60 Seconds)
1. **Show NEOC War Room:** Select a Pan-India theater (e.g. "Brahmaputra Flood" or "Himalayan Cloudburst"). Highlight the live multi-ministry telemetry and automated dam release directives.
2. **Switch to Citizen Hub:**
   - Select **हिंदी (Hindi)** or **తెలుగు (Telugu)** from the 8-language dropdown.
   - Hit **1-TAP SOS**: Phone sounds siren, screen flashes emergency strobe, and logs distress ticket.
   - Speak into the Voice SOS mic to demonstrate vernacular triage.
3. **Switch to NDRF & Armed Forces View:** Show the citizen's SOS ticket instantly appearing at the top of the national queue with a 95/100 vulnerability score. Click **"Authorize IAF Helicopter Sortie"** and **"Dispatch NDRF Zodiac Boat"**.
4. **Switch to Shelter Hub:** Show available highland beds, drinking water buffer, and search the national family reunification registry.
